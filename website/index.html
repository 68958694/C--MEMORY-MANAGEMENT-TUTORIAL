FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to us directly.

Who is career counselling for?

School and college students choosing a stream, course or college; graduates and professionals planning a career change; and anyone returning to work after a break.

Can parents join the sessions?

Yes. Our parent + student consultation mode brings the whole family into the conversation, so everyone feels confident about the decision.

What is a pre-counselling session?

It's your first step with us. Fill in the short pre-counselling form, and we'll get to know your goals and recommend the right next steps for you.

Which countries can you help me study in?

The United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, Germany, the rest of Europe, Malaysia and Singapore.

Do you help with admissions in India too?

Yes. We guide admissions to leading universities and colleges across India, as well as accredited online programmes.

Do you help with IELTS, SOPs and visas?

Yes. IELTS preparation, Statement of Purpose support and visa guidance are all part of our study-abroad services.
